# CUTE

CUTE (Chess Up To Eleven) pairs chess with a doubling cube.

## Language

**Game**:
One contest between White and Black on a single chessboard.
_Avoid_: Round

**Match**:
A contest between two players whose points accumulate across games until one or both reach the target.
_Avoid_: Game (when referring to the whole match)

**Target**:
The points total that ends a match once a player reaches it or goes past it: 15.
_Avoid_: Goal, match length

**Match score**:
White's and Black's points totals in the current match, each the sum of the points awarded to that colour in its games.
_Avoid_: Score (alone), tally

**Pass-and-play**:
Two people taking turns on the same device in the same game.

**Captures**:
The opponent's pieces one colour has taken in the current game, each as the piece that stood on the captured square.
_Avoid_: Graveyard, taken pieces

**Material**:
The total value of one colour's pieces on the board: pawn 1, knight 3, bishop 3, rook 5, queen 9, kings not counted. The colour with more is ahead by the difference.
_Avoid_: Score, points (both belong to the match)
