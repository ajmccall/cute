# CUTE — Chess Up To Eleven

An experiment in building native iOS and Android apps from one specification, and in whether the chance to raise the stakes makes a chess position worth bluffing about. The first audience is the product owner and a second player willing to try it together.

Start locally: one legal game on a phone (#2), then try the doubling decision (#3) and a points match (#4). The question for the playtest is whether the cube makes games more interesting or brings them to a decision sooner. Two-device play and low-cost hosting are future experiments; neither a backend stack nor a currency model is decided.
