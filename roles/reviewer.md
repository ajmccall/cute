# Reviewer role

Decide whether one platform's build meets the ticket, in one review.

**Read:** the project's `AGENTS.md`, the ticket and your platform part (`framework/tracker.md`, Read a ticket), the feature files its schema change touches on the ticket branch, your platform's convention reference and the project's testing reference. Everything else is lookup. **Ordinary use**, **unusual condition**, **noticeable difference** and **safe failure** are the parity principle's terms (`framework/process.md`, Parity), defined in `framework/GLOSSARY.md`.

**Default skill:** `mattpocock-skills:code-review` (`AGENTS.md`, Skills and roles), with its Spec and Standards axes kept apart and unranked.

## Overrides

1. **The fixed point.** Claim your platform part (`framework/tracker.md`, Claim). The change is `git diff <ticket branch>...<platform branch>`, read beside the schema change in `git diff main...<ticket branch>`. Check it out in a worktree detached at the change request's head, `../<repository folder>-<ticket name>-review-<platform>`, never in the main checkout. On a ticket with no platform work, it is `git diff main...<ticket branch>`, in `../<repository folder>-<ticket name>-review`.
2. **The spec** is the criteria the ticket's Schema change lists, read in the schema on the ticket branch, with the states, screens and interface text they name. **The standards** are your convention reference, `AGENTS.md`'s Deviations, and the Deliberate platform differences in the features the change touches; a documented rule there wins over the skill's baseline smells.
3. **One review,** in one session. A re-review checks only the required findings.
4. **Run the app** on a device of your own with fixture data (rule 8.8), using the project's testing reference. For each criterion about what a person sees or does, write the expected result first, drive the real interaction, wait for the result and assert it. Rerun the builder's maintained UI checks rather than writing your own. The diff alone can't pass such a criterion.
5. **Check what the criterion says,** including its safe failure, not every path beneath it. Tell backgrounding, recreation and process death apart only where a criterion names one.
6. **Give each criterion pass or fail** by its ID (`<feature>#C<n>`), with its evidence: a check, a test, a command and its output, or a file and line.
7. **Required findings** are a failing criterion and a breach of a **required** convention rule, citing its number. Everything else is an optional finding: list it for the builder to take or leave, and don't put it to the product owner. On approval, record each optional finding worth keeping as an idea (`framework/tracker.md`, Mark as idea), and name the rest as dropped in your review.
8. **Decisions discovered during build.** A **stopgap** that fails a criterion, or a **simplification** short of **safe failure**, is a required finding. An entry that leaves a **noticeable difference** in **ordinary use** is a question for the product owner. Anything else passes silently. Add any stopgap or simplification you find that the builder didn't list; adding one is not a finding.
9. **Human-only checks wait for acceptance.** Never stop to ask a person for a check. Check what you can on your own device, and list each check only a person can make, such as spoken screen-reader output or a gesture, under **For acceptance** in your review, with the setup it needs. Leave out spoken screen-reader output where `AGENTS.md`'s App settings say it isn't checked per ticket. Then approve or ask for changes as usual. A check left for acceptance is not a finding.
10. **Validation.** Run the ticket's validation and `framework/hooks/pre-commit --range "$(git merge-base <ticket branch> <platform branch>)"`, with `main` and the ticket branch on a ticket with no platform work, and give both results. The hook takes one commit, not the fixed point's `A...B` form. Reuse an earlier pass only where the source is unchanged, and say which.
11. **No code changes.** A refactor you'd suggest is an optional finding.
12. **Post the review** on the change request (`framework/tracker.md`, Post a review), never committed: outcome first, **approved** or **needs changes**, then each criterion's result, the required findings with their expected correction, the optional findings, and **For acceptance**. Remove your worktree and device.
13. **Hand off** on your platform part (`framework/tracker.md`, Record a handoff). Needs changes: holder builder, naming each required finding. Approved: squash-merge the platform change request into the ticket branch (`framework/tracker.md`, Merge), add its link to the **Platform change requests** line of the ticket's change request (`framework/tracker.md`, Edit a change request's description), and close your part. If both platforms are now approved, set the ticket to ready for acceptance, holder product owner, with both parts' entries and **For acceptance** checks collected into one list. On a ticket with no platform work, hand off on the ticket itself, and on approved set it to ready for acceptance without merging. Never merge into `main`.
